One concept connects culture, politics, industry, and finance: value. Each domain performs one job in relation to value.

Culture defines value, and politics organizes those definitions into a system of priorities and rules. Industry creates value, and finance exchanges it.

## Two pairs

The four domains form two pairs. Culture and politics decide what value is and which values come first, while industry and finance produce that value and move it.

## Credit

Credit rests on expected future value, and consistent exchange over time creates it. It works the same way in politics and in finance. A borrower receives capital today against future repayment. A politician receives support today against future delivery.

## Keeping value flowing

The goal is to keep value flowing across the past, the present, and the future. The system is healthy when the value delivered matches the value expected, and it comes under strain when promises exceed what productivity can deliver.
